## Location
The Town Hall will be located in the Jocelyn Bell Burnell room of the Schuster Building at The University of Manchester

You can find the Schuster Building on google maps [here](https://maps.app.goo.gl/w1DwXkvbASVrKm1fA).

The building is on the Manchester Oxford Road campus close to the city centre, so is readily accessible by public transport and walking is possible. More information about travelling the Schuster Building can be found [here](https://www.physics.manchester.ac.uk/about/maps-and-travel/)

## Local Information

### Accommodation
The venue is located on the University's Oxford Road campus. Many hotels in the South of the city centre will be within easy commuting distance, either walking or on Manchester's excellent bus system.

There are also several appropriate hotels on Oxford Road itself.

### Arrival
On Tuesday morning there will be tea and coffee available during the registration period from 10:30 to 11:00 am.
